## FARMER — "Will I get paid, and will a stranger believe I'm real?"

| Stage | Goal | Experience | Trust beat | Success |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Arrive** | Get in | OAuth sign-in `[X-01]` | "This looks legitimate and safe" | Signed in |
| **2. Onboard & learn** | Understand + set up | Conversational welcome → intent → **education (how trust works, honest limits)** → role emerges as Farmer `[X-02–07]` | "This is built for me, and it's honest with me" | Knows how the platform works; role confirmed |
| **3. Get verified** | Become trusted | Verification handoff that **explains what happens to my ID** → pending → verified `[X-08/09, F-02]` | **Anxiety → reassurance** (handing over an ID to strangers) | Verified badge; trust seeded |
| **4. First listing** | Sell | Capture produce (photo/video, crop, qty, price) → **see how it'll appear as an Opportunity** → publish `[F-03–05]` | Pride + control over how I'm represented | A live listing |
| **5. First order** | Complete a sale | Order arrives → order detail → fulfill `[F-08/09]` | "A stranger I never met just bought from me" | Order fulfilled |
| **6. Get paid** | Receive money (the #1 fear) | Payment lands in **Ledger** → request payout → M-Pesa `[F-10/11]` | **Relief** — the core promise kept | Money received |
| **7. Build reputation** | Grow | Reviews received → **Trust Score detail** (how it's built, how to improve, honest) `[F-12/13]` | "My reputation travels now" | Rising tier |
| **8. Thrive (ongoing)** | Do more | Price intelligence, group orders, AI assistant `[F-14–17]` | Empowered, not dependent | Repeat sales |

## Cross-role truths (the experience that must be consistent everywhere)

- **The high-anxiety moments are the same shape across roles** — handing over identity/evidence, committing money, awaiting a verdict. Each gets **status clarity + an anxiety-reducing illustration + honest limits**.
- **Trust is felt at every stage**, not just shown on one screen — legitimacy at sign-in, honesty in onboarding, reassurance at verification, confidence before payment, attribution at decisions.
- **Every role's onboarding is the same reframed experience** (teach → listen → role last), with a role-specific tail.
- **Open questions (Foundation §16) touch every journey** — Trust-Score comprehension, language, employer expectations — and stay flagged, not assumed, until validated.
